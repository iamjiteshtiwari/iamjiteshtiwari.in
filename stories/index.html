My stories

A Record of Experiences.

Stories are different from my professional work and thoughts. These are personal, they might be about something that has happened to me, something I remember or something that I just want to share.

I don't want to limit this section to one particular type of story.

My work experiences, learning experiences, personal experiences, travel, people I've met, events that have occurred in my life, or experiences that have taught me something.

There may be some short stories. Some may be longer. Some may have a definite lesson, others may just be a reminder of something I need to keep in mind.

Experiences

All experiences are part of the journey.

There are good experiences, bad experiences and unexpected experiences.

I feel that sometimes things can be learnt from experience which are not learnt from books or courses.

It also helps me to reflect on the event and what I have learned from the experience when I write about them.

Memories

There are certain things to remember.

Sometimes something may not seem like a lot at the time but when I reflect on it later it is.

I want this section to be a place for me to write some of those moments so that they don't fade from my mind.

Lessons from life

Not all lessons have to be based on a project.

Lessons are learned from errors, discussions, relationships, assignments, work and daily experiences.

I might post these lessons here as part of my own document and development.

Personal stories

It's my personal space.

The stories in this book are my experiences and my memories.

There may be other persons or incidents told in some stories in my life. I will post them with due respect and context.

This section will be expanded.

This section does not have a set plan.

I can't say how many stories I will write.

I can add things here as I learn them, as I remember them and for something to write about.

The old stories will continue to exist as part of my personal and as a testament to the journey.
