Stories

Experiences, experiments, and lessons.

A personal space for stories that are better understood through experience than through a list of skills or opinions.

Why stories?

Some things make more sense as a story.

A resume tells you what I have done. A project page can show what I built. A thoughts page can explain what I think. Stories can capture the journey between those things—the experience, mistakes, experiments, decisions, and lessons.

This section is for personal storytelling and does not need to follow the format of a professional case study.

Story types

What may appear here.

Experiences

Things I've experienced

Personal experiences that shaped how I work, learn, communicate, or see a problem.

Experiments

Things I tried

Small experiments with technology, tools, ideas, workflows, or new ways of doing something.

Lessons

What I learned

Practical lessons from successes, mistakes, unexpected results, and situations that changed my approach.

Journey

Personal progress

Stories about learning, career development, building projects, and the process of becoming better at something.

Future archive

A place to document the journey.

As more stories are written, this page can become an archive with individual story pages, dates, categories, tags, featured stories, and related projects or learning notes.

The aim is to keep the writing human and useful rather than turning every experience into a polished professional achievement.
